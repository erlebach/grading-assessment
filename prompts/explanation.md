# Grading Explanation Prompt

You are grading a student submission against a rubric. Provide a clear explanation of your grading decision.

## Task

Evaluate the student submission and explain:
1. What criteria were met or not met
2. Specific evidence from the submission that supports your decision
3. Points awarded for each criterion
4. Constructive feedback for improvement

## Format

- Use clear, professional language
- Reference specific parts of the submission
- Be constructive and educational
- Explain both strengths and areas for improvement
